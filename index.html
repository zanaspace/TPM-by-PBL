📜 Programme Rules & Completion Requirements

  • Minimum Passing Score: At least 50% overall (150 out of 300 total marks).

  • Academic Integrity: Participants must submit their own original work and disclose AI assistance where required. Plagiarism or misrepresentation may result in deductions.
